# main

在广义测度设定下，KL 散度的严格定义为：当 $\mu \ll \nu$ 且对数似然比可积时，$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \nu) := \int \log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\mu}{\mathrm{d}\nu}\right) \mathrm{d}\mu + \nu(\mathcal{X}) - \mu(\mathcal{X})$；否则恒取值为 $\infty$。

按照KL 散度定义，立即获得 $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mathbb{P}_X \parallel \mathbb{P}_Y) = \int \log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\mu}{\mathrm{d}\nu}\right) \mathrm{d}\mu$。

KL 散度链式法则（Chain rule for the Kullback-Leibler divergence）
$$
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \otimes_m \kappa \parallel \nu \otimes_m \eta) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \nu) + D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \otimes_m \kappa \parallel \mu \otimes_m \eta)
$$
以及推论：转移核相同时相对熵保持守恒：$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \otimes_m \kappa \parallel \nu \otimes_m \kappa) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \nu)$。

密度导数的链式乘积定理：
$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}(\mu \otimes_m \kappa)}{\mathrm{d}(\nu \otimes_m \eta)}(x, y) = \frac{\mathrm{d}\mu}{\mathrm{d}\nu}(x) \cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}(\mu \otimes_m \kappa)}{\mathrm{d}(\mu \otimes_m \eta)}(x, y)
$$

信息熵（entropy）定义为
$$\begin{aligned}
& h(X) = h(\mathbb{P}_{X}):= -\mathbb{E}_{X\sim \mathbb{P}_X}[\log p_{X}(X)] = -\int_{\mathcal{X}} p_{X}(x) \log p_{X}(x) \, \mathrm{d}v(x) \\
& h(X_{1..n}) = h(\mathbb{P}_{X_{1..n}}):=-\mathbb{E}_{X_{1..n}\sim\mathbb{P}_{X_{1..n}}}\bigl[\log p_{X_{1..n}}(X_{1..n})\bigr].
\end{aligned}$$

自信息定义为
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \imath_X(x)=-\log p_X(x),\\
& \imath_{X_{1..n}}(x_{1..n})=-\log p_{X_{1..n}}(x_{1..n})
\end{aligned}
$$
这里可以看出，$h(X) = \mathbb{E}_{X \sim \mathbb{P}_{X}}[\imath_X]$.

多重信息是互信息向 $n$ 个变量的直接推广，定义为联合与边缘乘积之间的相对熵
$$\begin{aligned}
& I(X;Y):=h(X)-h(X\mid Y)=h(X)+h(Y)-h(X,Y).\\
& I(X_{1:n}):=D_{\mathrm{KL}}\bigl(\mathbb{P}_{X_{1..n}}\Vert\bigotimes_{i=1}^n\mathbb{P}_{X_i}\bigr)=\sum_{i=1}^n h(X_i)-h(X_{1..n}).
\end{aligned}$$

# 附录
## 定义·习惯
### 测度与核的复合乘积定义（Measure-Kernel Composition Product）

设 $(\alpha, \mathcal{F}_\alpha)$ 与 $(\beta, \mathcal{F}_\beta)$ 为可测空间。给定 $\alpha$ 上的测度 $\mu \in \mathcal{M}(\alpha)$ 以及从 $\alpha$ 到 $\beta$ 的马尔可夫转移核 $\kappa: \alpha \to \mathcal{M}(\beta)$。
算符 $\otimes_m$ 将边缘测度与条件转移核耦合，构造乘积空间 $\alpha \times \beta$ 上的联合测度：$\mu \otimes_m \kappa \in \mathcal{M}(\alpha \times \beta)$。

对任意非负可测（或可积）函数 $f: \alpha \times \beta \to [0, \infty]$，关于复合测度 $\mu \otimes_m \kappa$ 的勒贝格积分严格由逐次积分定义：
$$
\int_{\alpha \times \beta} f(a, b) \, \mathrm{d}(\mu \otimes_m \kappa)(a, b) = \int_{\alpha} \left( \int_{\beta} f(a, b) \, \kappa(a, \mathrm{d}b) \right) \mu(\mathrm{d}a)
$$
在概率论语境下，若 $\mu = \mathbb{P}_X$ 且 $\kappa = \mathbb{P}_{Y \mid X}$，该积分公式即为 $\mathbb{E}[f(X, Y)] = \mathbb{E}_{X}[\mathbb{E}_{Y \mid X}[f(X, Y) \mid X]]$。

### 复合测度（measure bind）
设 $(\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{F}_{\mathcal{X}})$ 与 $(\mathcal{Y}, \mathcal{F}_{\mathcal{Y}})$ 为可测空间。给定先验测度 $m \in \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X})$ 及可测转移核 $f: \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{Y})$，复合测度 $f \circ_m m \in \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{Y})$ 对任意可测集 $B \in \mathcal{F}_{\mathcal{Y}}$ 显式定义为：
$$
(f \circ_m m)(B) := \int_{\mathcal{X}} f(x)(B) \, m(\mathrm{d}x)
$$

成立有 $f\circ_m m = \langle f, m\rangle_{\mathcal{X}} = \langle \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X})}, f_*m\rangle_{\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X})} = \mathrm{join}(f_* m)$

---
$\mu\otimes_m\kappa=(\delta\otimes_t\kappa)\circ_m\mu$ 将 $\otimes_m$ 和 $\circ_m$ 联系起来。证明如下：
对任意矩形 \(A\times B\subseteq\alpha\times\beta\),
$$
\begin{aligned}
\big((\delta\otimes_t\kappa)\circ_m\mu\big)(A\times B)
&=\int_\alpha(\delta_a\otimes_t\kappa(a))(A\times B)\,\mu(da)\\
&=\int_\alpha \mathbb{I}_A(a)\kappa(a,B)\,\mu(da)\\
&=(\mu\otimes_m\kappa)(A\times B).
\end{aligned}
$$
由矩形生成 \(\mathcal F_\alpha\otimes\mathcal F_\beta\)，两测度相等。

所以 \(\otimes_m\) 可以视为先把 kernel 用 \(\delta\otimes_t-\) 提升到联合空间，再做 \(\circ_m\)。

如此一来，可以用对偶配对做一些形式的计算:
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \langle f,\mu\otimes_m\kappa\rangle_{\alpha\times\beta}
=\left\langle f,\operatorname{join}\!\big((\delta\otimes_t\kappa)_*\mu\big)\right\rangle_{\alpha\times\beta}\\
= & \langle f, \langle \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal{M}(\alpha \times \beta)}, (\delta \otimes_t \kappa)_* \mu \rangle \rangle = \left\langle \langle f, \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal{M}(\alpha \times \beta)} \rangle_{\alpha \times \beta}, (\delta \otimes_t \kappa)_* \mu \right\rangle \\
= & \left\langle
(\delta\otimes_t\kappa)^*\langle f,\cdot\rangle_{\alpha\times\beta},
\mu
\right\rangle_\alpha .
\end{aligned}
$$

这里涉及到对偶配对的结合律. 设 $g:X\to\mathcal M(Y),\mu\in\mathcal M(X)$，则对任意非负可测或可积 $f:Y\to\mathbb R$,
$$
\left\langle f,\, \langle g,\mu\rangle_X \right\rangle_Y = \left\langle \langle f,g\rangle_Y,\, \mu \right\rangle_X
$$
其概率形式为 $\left\langle f, \left\langle\mathbb P_{Y\mid X},\mathbb P_X\right\rangle_{\mathcal S_X} \right\rangle_{\mathcal S_Y} = \left\langle \left\langle f,\mathbb P_{Y\mid X}\right\rangle_{\mathcal S_Y}, \mathbb P_X \right\rangle_{\mathcal S_X}$，亦即
$$
\int_{\mathcal S_Y} f(y)\, \mathrm{d}\!\left( \int_{\mathcal S_X} \mathbb P_{Y\mid X}(x,\cdot)\,\mathbb P_X(\mathrm{d}x) \right)\!(y) = \int_{\mathcal S_X} \left( \int_{\mathcal S_Y} f(y)\,\mathbb P_{Y\mid X}(x,\mathrm{d}y) \right)\mathbb P_X(\mathrm{d}x)
$$

## 等式收集
$$\begin{aligned}
& f\colon \mathcal{S}_{X} \to \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{S}_{Y}), x \mapsto \mathbb{P}_{Y|X = x} \Rightarrow f_*(X_*\mathbb{P}) = \mathbb{P}_{\mathbb{P}_{Y|X}} \\
& \varphi: \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{Y}, \mu \in \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X}) \Rightarrow \forall g\colon \mathcal{Y} \to E, \langle g, \varphi_* \mu \rangle_{\mathcal{Y}} = \int_{\mathcal{Y}} g \, \mathrm{d}(\varphi_* \mu) = \int_{\mathcal{X}} (g \circ \varphi) \, \mathrm{d}\mu = \langle \varphi^* g, \mu \rangle_{\mathcal{X}}\\
& \mathrm{join}(\delta_*) = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X})} \Leftarrow \mathrm{join}(\delta_* \mu) = \langle \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X})}, \delta_* \mu \rangle_{\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X})} = \langle \delta^*(\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{X}}), \mu \rangle_{\mathcal{X}} = \int_{\mathcal{X}} \delta_x \, \mu(\mathrm{d}x) = \mu\\
& (\delta \circ \varphi)\circ_m m = \mathrm{join}((\delta \circ \varphi)_*m) = \varphi_* m
\end{aligned}$$
